Privacy Policy for Arrow Breaker

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Arrow Breaker ("the app") is a puzzle game published by Misal Interactive ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, items, statistics and settings) is saved on your device. We do not run our own accounts or servers and we do not receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes the data stored on your device.

Google Play Games Services

The app can use Google Play Games Services to sign you in and to record your achievements. Signing in is optional. If you sign in, Google processes your Play Games profile (such as your player ID and display name) and the achievements you unlock, under Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We do not receive your name or email address. You can sign out, or delete your Play Games data, from the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information such as model, operating system and diagnostics,
  • how you interact with ads.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent before ads are requested, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice in the app's privacy options when available. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's Google settings. Learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Crash and performance data

Google Play may provide us with anonymous crash and performance reports through Android vitals.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location. We do not include analytics or social features of our own.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and contact

To ask a question or request help, email misalinteractive96@gmail.com. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
